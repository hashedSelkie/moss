Animals

ROE DEER
(capreolus capreolus)

Groups of roe deer are seen regularly on the Moss.

The smallest of Scotland’s deer, they are recognised primarily by their white rumps.

Their coats are red-brown in summer fading to dull brown in winter, and their preferred habitat is woodland, where they live on a diet of tree shoots, leaves, heather and blueberries.

FOSSORIAL VOLES

A relatively new arrival to the Moss is the colony of fossorial voles, discovered by accident during a bird survey.

One of only 2 colonies in Scotland, these are a protected species under the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981.

A full article on the voles can be found in our winter 2021/22 Newsletter (ABOUT US page), and ongoing works under Streetscene Technical Report Update June 2023 (HOME page).

COMMON FROG
(Rama Temporaria)

In Spring the ditches alongside Bea’s Path are full of frogspawn, and the frog remains one of the regular inhabitants of the Moss.
